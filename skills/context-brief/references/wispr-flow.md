# Wispr Flow setup (talk instead of type)

Wispr Flow is a dictation app. You hold a key, speak, let go, and it types what you said wherever your cursor is. That means it works straight into Claude, Codex, ChatGPT, your email or anywhere else you can type, with no plugin.

We are not affiliated with Wispr Flow. It's simply what we use. Any dictation tool works with this brief.

## Set it up

1. Download it from [wisprflow.ai](https://wisprflow.ai). It's available on Mac, Windows, iPhone and Android.
2. Open the app where you want to write (for example Claude or Codex) and click into the text box so the cursor is showing.
3. Press and hold the push-to-talk shortcut, speak, then let go. Your text appears a moment later.

## The keys

| | Mac | Windows |
|---|---|---|
| Push to talk (hold while you speak) | **Fn** | **Ctrl + Win** |
| Hands-free (no holding) | **Fn + Space** | **Ctrl + Win + Space** |

On a Mac without an Apple Fn key, push to talk is **Ctrl + Opt** and hands-free is **Ctrl + Opt + Space**. You can also start hands-free by double-pressing the push-to-talk key quickly. You can change the keys in Wispr Flow's Settings, under General, then Shortcuts.

## What it costs

There's a free plan with no trial countdown and no card needed. On the free plan you get 2,000 words a week on desktop and 1,000 a week on mobile. Paid plans give unlimited dictation; see [wisprflow.ai/pricing](https://wisprflow.ai/pricing) for current prices.

## Tips for a good talk-through

- Talk like you're briefing a new starter, not writing an email.
- Use hands-free mode for long briefs so you can walk around and think.
- Don't worry about ums or going back on yourself. Claude tidies it up.
- Don't dictate passwords, card numbers or anything you wouldn't want in a chat.
